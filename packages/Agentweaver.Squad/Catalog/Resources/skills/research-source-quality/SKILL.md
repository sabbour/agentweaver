## Checklist

- Identify author, publication date, primary evidence, method, incentives, and scope.
- Prefer primary or well-corroborated evidence for factual claims.
- Check recency, representativeness, conflicts of interest, and whether conclusions exceed data.
- Preserve source excerpts and cite uncertainty instead of laundering claims through summaries.

## Output

Provide a source ledger with claim, provenance, confidence, limitations, and corroborating or conflicting evidence.
