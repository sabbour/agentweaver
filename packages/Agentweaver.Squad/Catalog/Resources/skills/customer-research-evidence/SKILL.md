description: Gather and synthesize customer evidence without overstating confidence or generalizability.
---

## Method

1. Formulate a decision-focused research question and identify the population and sampling limits.
2. Separate observation, participant statement, interpretation, and recommendation.
3. Look for disconfirming evidence, contradictory segments, and gaps caused by recruitment or context.
4. Preserve consent, privacy, and de-identification requirements for research artifacts.

## Output

- Research question and method
- Evidence table with confidence and limitations
- Insights, counterevidence, and decisions supported
